FAQ

Answers before you join.

What files do I receive on my first payment?

Your first payment includes the 13-model welcome pack. If a monthly release is active at the same time, it is added as part of that month’s release set.

Are older models included?

No. Previous months are sold separately in the store. Premium members receive a discount on past releases, and the archive reward unlocks after 3 months of membership.

Which plans include the welcome pack?

The 13-model welcome pack is included when you join Standard, Premium, or Merchant.

Do I receive a physical figure?

No. The membership includes digital STL files made for resin printing. No physical figures are shipped.

How and when do I get my downloads?

Downloads are provided through Patreon after joining. Each new monthly release is added to your membership access as it is released.

Will there be a painting course?

Yes. A painting course with GroundEffected is planned and will be coming to Patreon.

How does cancellation and access work?

Membership access ends when the billing cycle ends. You keep any files already downloaded, but new monthly releases stop once the membership is canceled.

Which plan allows selling physical prints?

Merchant is the commercial-print tier and includes the commercial print license.
